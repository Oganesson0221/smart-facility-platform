# Fire Exit Obstruction

Confirm the affected exit and review the recorded evidence. Notify Facilities
Security with the incident identifier and obstruction description. Keep people
away from the obstructed route and direct them to the nearest approved alternate
exit. Do not move a vehicle or heavy obstruction without an authorised operator.

If the obstruction has not been removed within five minutes, escalate to the duty
facility manager. Record the acknowledgement, resolution time, and any false alarm.
