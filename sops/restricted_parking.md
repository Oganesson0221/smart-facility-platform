# Restricted Parking and Access Zone

Confirm that visible signs or road markings identify the area as restricted and
that the vehicle is inside that area. Record the camera, location, incident
identifier, and only vehicle details visible in the supplied evidence.

Notify Facilities Security to locate the authorised driver and request that the
vehicle be moved through normal site procedures. Do not enter, tow, or move the
vehicle without authorisation. If it remains after ten minutes or restricts
operational access, escalate to the duty facility manager. Record acknowledgement,
resolution time, and any false alarm.
